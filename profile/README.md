## ⚡ Dynamax & Gigantamax

One of the defining mechanics in **Pokémon Sword** is **Dynamax**. During certain battles, Pokémon can temporarily become enormous and gain access to powerful Max Moves.

Some Pokémon have access to **Gigantamax**, a special transformation that changes their appearance and gives them access to unique G-Max Moves.

These mechanics play an important role in major Gym battles and other important encounters throughout the Galar adventure.

## 🌳 The Wild Area

The **Wild Area** is one of the game's most distinctive locations. It provides a large open environment where players can encounter many different Pokémon.

Weather conditions can affect which Pokémon appear, encouraging players to return to different areas at different times.

The Wild Area also contains **Max Raid Battles**, where players can team up with other Trainers to battle powerful Dynamax Pokémon.

## 🏆 Gym Challenge

The Gym Challenge is the central progression system in **Pokémon Sword**.

Players travel throughout Galar and challenge Gym Leaders specializing in different Pokémon types. Defeating a Gym Leader rewards the player with a Gym Badge and brings them closer to the Pokémon League.

Gym battles are presented as major sporting events, giving the Galar region a unique stadium-focused atmosphere.
